Deploy and manage the LaunchNFT contracts from a phone wallet. Open this page in your wallet's built-in browser (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet…).

Not connected.

1. Deploy contracts

Your connected wallet becomes the owner. It deploys the Registry, allow-lists Seaport 1.6, then the raffle contract, two templates and the Launcher on top of Pons V2. That is up to 6 small transactions (about 0.0006 ETH in total). If it stops, tap Deploy again and it continues where it left off.

    2. Manage registry

    Set launcher in config.js (or deploy above) to manage the registry.

    3. Other chains (Ethereum, Base, BNB)

    Deploys the vault template and launcher used for collections outside Robinhood Chain. That is 2 small transactions, each under 1.1M gas. It reuses your Registry and Raffles.

      After listing, add the collection to collections.json on GitHub (the snippet appears below) so the site and keeper know its name and slug.
